Sitter Boss - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 2026

Welcome to Sitter Boss (“we”, “our”, “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Profile details (bio, experience, etc.)
  • Profile images

b. Location Information

  • Real-time or approximate location for job matching and navigation

c. Booking & Usage Data

  • Booking details, schedules, preferences
  • App usage behavior and interactions

d. Payment Information

  • Payments are processed securely via third-party providers (e.g., Stripe, Razorpay)
  • We do NOT store your card details

e. AI-Generated Content

  • Information you provide to generate bios or content using AI tools

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and manage babysitting services
  • Match sitters with parents
  • Process bookings and payments
  • Improve app features and user experience
  • Generate AI-based content
  • Send notifications and updates

3. Sharing of Information

  • Other users (e.g., sitter profiles visible to parents)
  • Payment providers (for secure transactions)
  • Service providers (analytics, hosting, etc.)

We do NOT sell your personal data.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

  • Update or delete your profile
  • Request data deletion
  • Opt out of notifications

6. Third-Party Services

  • Google Maps (location services)
  • Payment gateways
  • Analytics tools

These services have their own privacy policies.

7. Children’s Privacy

Sitter Boss is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted with an updated date.
